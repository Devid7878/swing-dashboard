Pre-trade checklist

34 rules

Market & selection
  1. Decide regime before deciding to trade.
  2. Green normal · Yellow half · Orange minimal · Red none.
  3. Only proven swing setups.
  4. Only the Focus List generates trades.
  5. No valid setup = no trade.
  6. No random intraday trades.
  7. IPOs only on my predefined setup.
  8. Choppy: cut exposure, raise selectivity, not frequency.
  9. Protect capital in bad markets, deploy in good ones.
  10. Never lower standards on an empty list.
Risk & sizing
  1. Entry, stop, size, risk set before buying.
  2. Size from stop + risk budget, not conviction.
  3. Never exceed per-trade risk.
  4. Never exceed max portfolio open risk.
  5. After a losing streak: cut size, review, resume.
  6. Stop initiating at the loss limit.
Execution
  1. Never chase a missed entry.
  2. No FOMO, boredom, excitement, revenge.
  3. No averaging down unless tested.
  4. Never widen a stop.
  5. Never sell a winner to offset a loser.
  6. Manage by setup, not today's P&L.
  7. Let winners run.
  8. Follow the system when emotions disagree.
Mindset
  1. A stop is a business expense.
  2. No need to trade daily or weekly.
  3. Cash is a position.
  4. Execute a positive-expectancy process.
  5. Miss a great trade over taking a bad one.
  6. Capital preservation first.
Measure & review
  1. Judge by R and expectancy, not ₹.
  2. Journal every trade incl. rule violations.
  3. Judge over a meaningful sample.
  4. Weekly review: regime, setup, execution or psychology?
