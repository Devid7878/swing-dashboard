Pre-trade checklist

Rules

🧭 Market & selection
  • Decide regime before deciding to trade.
  • Green normal · Yellow half · Orange minimal · Red none.
  • Only proven swing setups.
  • Only the Focus List generates trades.
  • No valid setup = no trade.
  • No random intraday trades.
  • IPOs only on my predefined setup.
  • Choppy: cut exposure, raise selectivity, not frequency.
  • Protect capital in bad markets, deploy in good ones.
  • Never lower standards on an empty list.
🛡️ Risk & sizing
  • Entry, stop, size, risk set before buying.
  • Size from stop + risk budget, not conviction.
  • Never exceed per-trade risk.
  • Never exceed max portfolio open risk.
  • After a losing streak: cut size, review, resume.
  • Stop initiating at the loss limit.
⚙️ Execution
  • Never chase a missed entry.
  • No FOMO, boredom, excitement, revenge.
  • No averaging down unless tested.
  • Never widen a stop.
  • Never sell a winner to offset a loser.
  • Manage by setup, not today's P&L.
  • Let winners run.
  • Follow the system when emotions disagree.
🧘 Mindset
  • A stop is a business expense.
  • No need to trade daily or weekly.
  • Cash is a position.
  • Execute a positive-expectancy process.
  • Miss a great trade over taking a bad one.
  • Capital preservation first.
📒 Measure & review
  • Judge by R and expectancy, not ₹.
  • Journal every trade incl. rule violations.
  • Judge over a meaningful sample.
  • Weekly review: regime, setup, execution or psychology?
